Create one beautiful, highly believable industrial-design concept image of a tiny walking robot made from a RayCue Maclock Macintosh-shaped alarm clock. The user wants an intelligent companion with a classic Mac OS 9 personality; vintage apps are secondary.

Subject: Keep the recognizable original miniature 1984 compact Macintosh enclosure, about 10 cm tall before legs: warm ivory/beige lightly textured ABS plastic, a tall box with a gently sloping upper front, integrated top carry-handle recess between two vented shoulders, slightly curved dark glass screen with deep chamfered ivory bezel, broad chin, tiny rainbow Apple badge at lower left and a narrow horizontal floppy slot with a little grey ejectable disk tab at lower right. This is a miniature Macintosh body, not a modern flat monitor or an iMac.

Add FOUR short, articulated walking legs mounted to a discreet compact base under the original computer body. Two legs per side, clearly anatomically coherent, each with two simple rounded ivory housings and a dark visible circular joint, ending in small rounded graphite rubber feet. Thoughtful plausible hobby-robot proportions; understated hardware and small fasteners, protected wiring, no exposed cable mess. The original Mac enclosure should remain the dominant silhouette. It should look charming, curious and capable, not militaristic, insectlike or bulky. Show it mid-step with one front foot slightly lifted and the remaining feet supporting it, slight friendly lean. No arms, no additional head, no ears, no tail, no wheels.

Screen: unmistakably CLASSIC MAC OS 9 PLATINUM interface, not modern macOS. Pale periwinkle blue desktop, thin light-grey menu bar across the top with a small rainbow Apple icon and readable menu labels 'File' 'Edit' 'View' 'Special'. A couple of tiny pixel-style desktop icons at the right, including a hard disk and Trash. One centered compact classic dialog with horizontal-striped grey title bar, black bitmap typography and a little black-and-white Happy Mac icon. Dialog text EXACTLY 'Hello, Bill.' with one small beveled button labeled 'Hello'. The screen stays a real OS desktop; do not replace the whole screen with cartoon eyes. Authentic low-resolution bitmap graphics, crisp enough to recognize and read. No Dock, no modern rounded traffic-light controls, no holograms.

Composition: one hero product view, full robot visible, front three-quarter angle, camera near robot screen height with a slight view of the top, all four legs clearly accounted for. The face and screen are prominent and leg geometry is legible. On a warm lightly grained wooden tabletop, softly blurred neutral interior background with no people and no clutter. Soft daylight and restrained studio fill, realistic contact shadows, warm nostalgic light, tactile plastic, precise joints and subtle glass reflection that does not hide the UI. Premium photorealistic product concept render, generous clean framing, a coherent physical object that could plausibly be built. Landscape composition. No diagram labels, no collage, no additional views, no watermark or promotional copy. This is a visual concept, not an engineering drawing.
