# Mack — public introduction

These approved captions describe the project's long-term goal. Resonance published
both updates on 2026-10-21 and verified them after reload. The X post is Public;
the Facebook post retained its Friends audience. Their wording distinguishes the
working desktop from future physical forms.

## Facebook caption

The long-term goal for Mack is to grow a personalized intelligence: a companion
who gets to know me through conversation, shared projects, preferences and
memories I choose to keep. I want her to build a history with me and become more
useful over time, while keeping a familiar personality as her models and tools
improve.

We're starting with M.A.C. Model 04: a little Mac-inspired robot with a classic
desktop, local intelligence and lasting memory. The desktop and local AI already
work on my Mac; the four-legged body is still being engineered.

Years from now, the goal is to carry that same Mack into a humanoid body for a
fuller interaction experience: talking, perceiving, gesturing, moving and helping
with everyday activities. Her remembered history and identity should travel with
her. Her voice and manner are central to that continuity.

We're building in public, from these first software foundations toward that
long-term goal: https://github.com/Bill-Viking/mack-model-04
