## What to look for at each stop

1. **Jameco.** The title reads "Benchtop Power Supply HY3005" and the page says
   "Input Voltage: 110VAC @ 60Hz", which is a US wall socket. Click **Add to cart**. Its
   outputs are banana jacks; if the box has no test leads, alligator-clip leads join the
   next Adafruit order.
2. **Adafruit.** Open each link and click **Add to Cart**; all nine go in one cart. The
   solder is lead-free on purpose, so the bench needs only a fan and an open window.
3. **PINE64.** Click **Add to cart** under "PINECIL – Smart Mini Portable Soldering Iron
   (Version 2)", then add the $3.99 cable from Related products. The iron runs from a
   USB-C laptop charger of 60 W or more, such as a MacBook Pro charger; a small 30 W
   charger is too weak. The tip set on that page ($24.99) is not needed: the iron comes
   with a tip.
