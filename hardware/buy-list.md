## What to look for at each stop

1. **ROBOTIS.** The page title reads "OpenRB-150 Starter Kit" and the price reads $52.79.
   Click **Add to cart**, then check out. Do not buy the "DYNAMIXEL Starter Set"; its
   supply is 12 V and this servo takes 5 V.
2. **American Weigh Scales.** The page reads "SC2KG PRECISION DIGITAL POCKET SCALE",
   $25.99, In Stock. Click **Add to Cart**, then check out.
3. **Penn Tool Co.** Skip this stop if a digital caliper is already on hand. The page
   reads "iGaging 6" IP54 Digital Caliper Absolute Origin - 100-700-06", $83.95. Click
   **Add to Cart**, then check out.
4. **Bambu Lab, two items in one order.**
   - Open the printer link. Beside the pictures the line must read
     **Selected: P2S × 1** and the price **$449.00**. If it reads "P2S Combo" or
     $649.00, click the small picture labelled **P2S**. Click **Add to Cart**, not
     "Buy Now", so the filament can join the same order.
   - Open the filament link. The line must read
     **Selected: Jade White (10100) / Filament with spool / 1kg**. If it reads
     "Refill", click **Filament with spool**; a refill has no spool and cannot be used
     alone. Click **Add to Cart**.
   - Open the cart, check for two lines (P2S and PLA Basic), then check out.

A USB-C data cable is also needed for the servo board. It is not in the kit.

## Notes

- The two Bambu links carry the store's own option number, so they open with the right
  option already chosen. Both were opened and read back that way on 2026-11-10.
- The first filament order is PLA only. PETG HF is marked discontinued on its page, and
  the PETG Basic page answered with the store's visitor check on two tries, so it was
  not read. PETG joins a later order, before the first load-carrying parts.
- The store lists PLA Basic with spool at $17.99 for one roll, with discounts from two
  rolls up. The cart shows the exact amount.
- The printer page states free shipping to the 48 contiguous states (see the
  [printer comparison](printer-comparison.md)). Shipping at the other three stores
  was not read.
- Reasons for each item: [first order](first-order.md) and
  [printer comparison](printer-comparison.md).
